# RybexOS Workflow Product Design Audit

## Purpose

This placeholder captures the current workflow product design standard until a deeper audit is written.

## Current Standard

RybexOS workflows must guide a user through a real business process, not only expose fields and completion buttons. A workflow should make the business object clear, explain why the item is blocked, capture required inputs and evidence references, gate downstream actions until required information is saved, produce an outcome record, and preserve a historical record that can support review, dispute, or future operating decisions.

## Current Gap

The existing workflow mechanics prove that local/demo completion can move states, save fields, and show outcome history. The next product-design step is to make the workflow itself read like the business process Rybex actually runs, with the measurable outcome and handoff made explicit before any new implementation work occurs.

## Billing v2 Direction

Billing v2 should be designed around Pay App readiness. The user should understand the blocked pay application, the cash impact, the required backup, the reviewer handoff, the final business outcome, and where the historical record lives. This file does not claim Billing v2 has been implemented.
