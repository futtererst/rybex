# 10-Minute Executive Demo Script

## Opening

“RybexOS is the operating system for running subcontracted infrastructure work
with discipline from pursuit through closeout. It does not just track projects;
it encodes how the work should be run.”

## 1. Command Center

- Click: `/command-center`
- Show: Executive banner, operating summary, leadership watch list, operating actions.
- Say: “This is the daily cockpit. Leadership can see what is active, what is blocked, where money is exposed, and what decision is needed next.”
- Proof point: D5O phase lanes and operating actions connect every module into one control model.
- Transition: “Now let’s start where margin protection begins: deciding what work Rybex should pursue.”

## 2. Pipeline

- Click: `/pipeline`
- Show: Go/no-go queue, high-risk opportunities, bid deadlines.
- Say: “Estimating resources should not be committed until the D1 pursuit gate is approved.”
- Proof point: Opportunities show fit, risk, score, recommendation, owner, and next action.
- Transition: “Once work is awarded, the system prevents unclear scope from reaching the field.”

## 3. Projects

- Click: `/projects`
- Show: Contract baseline, D2 exceptions, scope/budget/schedule readiness.
- Say: “D2 captures what Rybex agreed to deliver, what was excluded, what terms matter, and what must be resolved before mobilization.”
- Proof point: Contract, retainage, notice, change terms, scope matrix, and missing artifacts are visible.
- Transition: “With the baseline controlled, the next question is whether the field is actually ready.”

## 4. Mobilization

- Click: `/mobilization`
- Show: D3 readiness, blocked mobilizations, upcoming field starts, work package status.
- Say: “Crews should not mobilize until safety, access, permits, locates, materials, equipment, and work packages are ready.”
- Proof point: D3 gate readiness prevents unsafe or commercially exposed field starts.
- Transition: “Once field work starts, RybexOS captures proof as the work happens.”

## 5. Field Execution

- Click: `/field-execution`
- Show: Daily report compliance, work package execution, delays, issues, safety/quality signals.
- Say: “Daily reports protect production tracking, billing backup, change recovery, and closeout evidence.”
- Proof point: Changed conditions and extra work prompt RFIs or change events before notice windows are missed.
- Transition: “Those prompts become formal project-control records.”

## 6. RFIs/Submittals And Changes

- Click: `/rfis-submittals`, then `/changes`
- Show: Overdue RFIs, schedule-critical RFIs, submittals blocking work, change notice risks, missing backup.
- Say: “Field uncertainty and GC direction must become formal records, or margin leaks out of the job.”
- Proof point: RFI, submittal, change, notice, backup, pricing, and billing status are linked.
- Transition: “Commercial control only matters if it turns into clean payment.”

## 7. Billing

- Click: `/billing`
- Show: Cash at risk, missing backup, approved-not-billed changes, retainage, lien waivers.
- Say: “Billing is not generic invoicing here. It connects field proof, approved changes, contract terms, and backup documentation.”
- Proof point: Pay application readiness and commercial exposure are visible to PMs, finance, and leadership.
- Transition: “Safety and quality are also operating controls, not separate compliance islands.”

## 8. Safety And Quality

- Click: `/safety`, then `/quality`
- Show: JHAs, observations, incidents, corrective actions, inspections, tests, deficiencies, punch.
- Say: “Safety and quality records control field readiness, execution risk, billing exposure, and closeout evidence.”
- Proof point: Corrective actions, failed inspections, missing tests, and punch items are tied to project impact.
- Transition: “At the end, all evidence comes together in closeout.”

## 9. Closeout

- Click: `/closeout`
- Show: Closeout package readiness, missing as-builts/tests, final billing blockers, retainage blockers, acceptance queue.
- Say: “D5 turns completed work into acceptance, final billing readiness, retainage release, and archive-quality documentation.”
- Proof point: Closeout consumes field, quality, safety, commercial, RFI, submittal, and billing records.
- Transition: “The last step is how Rybex gets better on the next job.”

## 10. Optimize

- Click: `/reports`
- Show: Project scorecards, lessons learned, production rates, GC/vendor performance, improvement actions.
- Say: “Optimize closes the loop. Completed work updates estimating rates, go/no-go scoring, mobilization checklists, and partner posture.”
- Proof point: Lessons are assigned to owners and tied to operating-system changes.
- Transition: “The platform is also prepared for persistence, RBAC, and audit.”

## 11. Admin Readiness

- Click: `/admin`
- Show: Module maturity, route coverage, data source mode, RBAC, audit, docs.
- Say: “This is the readiness view for moving from seed-data prototype toward durable enterprise platform.”
- Proof point: Persistence, RBAC, audit, route safety, and demo documentation are already mapped.

## Close

“This is the difference between managing projects with spreadsheets and running
work through a controlled operating model.”
