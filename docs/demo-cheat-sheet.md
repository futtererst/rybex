# Demo Cheat Sheet

## Opening Line

“RybexOS is the operating system for running subcontracted infrastructure work
with discipline from pursuit through closeout. It does not just track projects;
it encodes how the work should be run.”

## Fast Page Guide

| Page | Click | Say | Show |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Command Center | `/command-center` | “This is the executive cockpit for risk, money, field blockers, gates, and decisions.” | Executive banner, metrics, phase lanes, operating actions. |
| Pipeline | `/pipeline` | “Rybex decides whether to pursue before estimating resources are committed.” | Go/no-go queue, bid deadlines, risk scores. |
| Projects | `/projects` | “Awarded work becomes a controlled D2 contract baseline.” | Scope matrix, payment/retainage/notice terms, D2 exceptions. |
| Mobilization | `/mobilization` | “D3 prevents crews from starting before the field is ready.” | Safety, access, permits, locates, materials, work packages. |
| Field Execution | `/field-execution` | “D4 captures proof and prompts formal escalation while work happens.” | Daily report compliance, production, changed conditions. |
| RFIs/Submittals | `/rfis-submittals` | “Questions and approvals are controlled before they block production.” | Overdue RFIs, rejected submittals, schedule-critical items. |
| Changes | `/changes` | “Extra work becomes notice, backup, pricing, and recovery.” | Notice risks, backup gaps, approved-not-billed changes. |
| Billing | `/billing` | “Completed work and approved changes become clean pay application readiness.” | Cash at risk, missing backup, retainage, lien waivers. |
| Safety | `/safety` | “Safety records control readiness, incidents, and corrective actions.” | JHAs, observations, incident follow-up, overdue actions. |
| Quality | `/quality` | “Quality records protect acceptance and closeout evidence.” | Inspections, deficiencies, tests, punch. |
| Closeout | `/closeout` | “D5 proves completion, final billing readiness, retainage release, and acceptance.” | Missing tests, as-builts, acceptance queue, blockers. |
| Optimize | `/reports` | “Completed work updates estimating rates, risk scoring, checklists, and partner posture.” | Scorecards, lessons, production rates, GC/vendor profiles. |
| Admin | `/admin` | “The prototype has enterprise hardening paths for persistence, RBAC, audit, and route safety.” | Module status, smoke routes, docs, known limits. |

## Seven Proof Points

1. D5O phase control.
2. Go/no-go discipline.
3. Contract baseline before mobilization.
4. Field readiness gate.
5. Daily field control.
6. Change, billing, and cash-risk visibility.
7. Closeout and optimization loop.

## Closing Line

“This is the difference between managing projects with spreadsheets and running
work through a controlled operating model.”
